Connect the switch

Home Assistant — switch Pixoo Screen (switch.pixoo_screen) appears automatically via MQTT discovery (Settings → Devices & Services → MQTT).
Apple Home — HA → enable HomeKit Bridge, include the switch, add it in the Apple Home app.
Alexa / Google — expose the switch via HA Cloud (Nabu Casa) or your existing link, then “turn Pixoo Screen off”.
